Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Altitude Capital Advisory LLC ("Altitude Capital", "we", "us") owns and manages residential rental properties in Michigan. This policy explains what personal information we collect from our tenants, rental applicants, contractors and visitors to altitudecap.com, how we use it, and who we share it with.

What personal information we collect

We collect your name, mobile phone number, email address and mailing address; your rental application, lease and rent payment records; and the messages and photos you send us. When you agree to receive text messages, we collect your name and mobile phone number and record your consent. In detail:

  • Contact details: name, mobile and other phone numbers, email address and mailing address.
  • Tenancy information: rental applications, leases and renewals, the unit you rent, move-in and move-out dates, rent payments and balances, and security deposit records.
  • Maintenance and communications: repair requests, photos you send us, and the text messages, emails and calls we exchange with you.
  • Contractors: name, business name, contact details and invoices.
  • Website visitors: our website does not ask you to create an account and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may record basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to operate the site securely. If you use the form on our SMS Terms page, we receive what you enter in it.

How we use your personal information

  • To manage your tenancy: leases, renewals, rent collection and records.
  • To arrange repairs, maintenance visits and city rental inspections, and to keep you informed about them.
  • To communicate with you by text message, email and phone about your tenancy or, for contractors, about repair work.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including city rental certification requirements, and to protect our rights.

We do not use your information for marketing or advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who we share your personal information with

  • Contractors and maintenance staff working at our properties, who may receive your name, unit address and phone number only so they can schedule and carry out a repair.
  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as our phone and texting provider, our bank and payment platforms you choose to pay rent through, email, document storage and accounting providers. They may use your information only to provide their services to us.
  • Government authorities such as city rental inspection departments, when required for rental certification or by law.
  • Others when required by law, for example in response to a court order, or to protect the safety of our tenants and properties.

None of the sharing described above includes text messaging opt-in data or consent. See the next section.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you agree to receive text messages from us, we use your mobile number only to text you about your tenancy or, for contractors, about repair work. How to sign up, what we text about and how often are explained in our SMS Terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or your text messaging opt-in data and consent.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP to 305-901-5302. Reply HELP for help.

How we protect and keep your information

We keep your information in access-controlled systems available only to the people who manage our properties, protected by multi-factor sign-in and encryption. We keep tenancy records for as long as you are a tenant and afterwards for as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or correct the information we hold about you, to delete it, or to stop text messages, by contacting us below. We will answer within 30 days. Some information must be kept to meet our legal obligations.
