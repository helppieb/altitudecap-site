Altitude Capital Advisory LLC ("Altitude Capital") owns and manages residential rental properties in Michigan. We text our tenants and contractors from 305-901-5302 about their tenancy or repair work. This page explains how to sign up, what we text about, and how to stop.

How to sign up for text messages

You can agree to receive text messages from Altitude Capital in any of these ways:

  1. Online: fill in the form below and tick the consent box.
  2. On paper: tick the text message consent box in your lease or on our tenant or contractor contact form. It uses the same consent wording shown below.
  3. By texting us first: text us at 305-901-5302 about your rental or a repair. You agree to receive our replies about that conversation, and you can reply STOP at any time.

Contact us and sign up for text messages

You can send us a message without agreeing to text messages. The consent box is never pre-checked.

What we text about

  • Tenants: conversations about your tenancy, including repair and maintenance requests and scheduling, city rental inspection appointments, utility or water shut-offs, rent reminders and payment confirmations, and lease renewals.
  • Contractors: scheduling and details of repair work at our properties.

We never send marketing or advertising messages.

Example messages

Altitude Capital: Hi Maria, the plumber will be at 80 Mark Ave. tomorrow between 9 and 11am to fix the kitchen sink. Reply STOP to opt out.

Altitude Capital: The City of Pontiac rental inspection for your unit is set for Tuesday, Dec 1 at 10am. Please make sure someone 18+ is home. Reply HELP for help.

The details

  • Message frequency varies depending on your requests and what is happening at your property.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To opt out at any time, reply STOP to 305-901-5302. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts. To start again, reply START.
  • For help, reply HELP to 305-901-5302, email info@altitudecap.com, or call 305-901-5302.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of renting from us. If you opt out, we will contact you by phone, email or mail instead.

  • Your mobile number and text message consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
