Altitude Capital Advisory LLC ("Altitude Capital") owns and manages residential rental properties in Michigan. Through our text message program, Altitude Capital Property Texts, we text our tenants and contractors from 305-901-5302 about their tenancy or repair work. This page explains how to sign up, what we text about, how often, and how to stop.

How to sign up for text messages

Text messages are only sent to people who have agreed to receive them. You can agree in any of these ways:

  1. Online: fill in the form below and tick the consent box. The box is optional and never pre-checked.
  2. On paper: sign our Text Message Consent Form, given to tenants at lease signing and to contractors when they start work. The same consent box and wording also appear in our leases.
  3. By texting us first: text us at 305-901-5302 about your rental or a repair. You agree to receive our replies about that conversation.

Every sign-up method shows the same consent wording, the same disclosures (message frequency, message and data rates, STOP and HELP), and links to these SMS Terms and our Privacy Policy. After you sign up, our first text confirms your enrollment and repeats how to stop.

Contact us and sign up for text messages

You can send us a message without agreeing to text messages. Leaving the box unticked means we will not text you.

What we text about

  • Tenants: conversations about your tenancy, including repair and maintenance requests and scheduling, city rental inspection appointments, utility or water shut-offs, rent reminders and payment confirmations, and lease renewals.
  • Contractors: scheduling and details of repair work at our properties.

We never send marketing, advertising or promotional messages, and we never send texts on behalf of anyone else.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your requests and what is happening at your property, typically a few messages a month.

Messages you will receive when you sign up, ask for help, or opt out

When you sign up: "Altitude Capital: You're signed up for texts from Altitude Capital Advisory LLC about your rental or repair work. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

When you reply HELP: "Altitude Capital Advisory LLC: For help, call or text 305-901-5302 or email info@altitudecap.com. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."

When you reply STOP: "Altitude Capital Advisory LLC: You are unsubscribed and will receive no further texts. Reply START to resubscribe."

Example messages

"Altitude Capital: Hi Maria, the plumber will be at 80 Mark Ave. tomorrow between 9 and 11am to fix the kitchen sink. Reply STOP to opt out."

"Altitude Capital: The City of Pontiac rental inspection for your unit is set for Tuesday, Dec 1 at 10am. Please make sure someone 18+ is home. Reply HELP for help."
